About & FAQ

We are on a mission to digitize the campus economy.

Is CU Marketplace free for students?

Yes! Browsing listings, comparing prices, and contacting vendors is 100% free for all students.

How do I verify if a vendor is genuine?

Look for the "VERIFIED" badge. This means our team has physically checked the vendor's shop and credentials.

Can I list my old books or gadgets?

Currently, we only allow registered local businesses. However, a peer-to-peer student exchange feature is coming soon!

How does the rating system work?

Ratings are crowd-sourced from students who have actually used the services. We monitor for spam reviews.

I am a vendor. How do I join?

Click on "Join Now" in the navbar, fill out the vendor application form, and our team will contact you within 24 hours.
